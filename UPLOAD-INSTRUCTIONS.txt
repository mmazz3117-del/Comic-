COMIC VAULT v10.57 — SIX APPROVED FRIDAY NEWSPAPERS

This replaces the held v10.55/v10.56 newspaper updates. Use only this v10.57 app ZIP and its matching Newspaper Images ZIP. Nothing has been deployed for you.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Friday the 13th (1980): six full illustrated newspapers in chronological order.
- Before 1957: Mrs. Voorhees as camp cook (explicitly fan-imagined).
- 1957 reported drowning; 1958 counselor killings; 1962 water setback.
- Steve Christy's reopening preparations; the original film's aftermath.
- 26 clickable printed articles. Open a story to enlarge the actual newspaper image; switch to Readable text for its exact transcript. Back returns to the same full paper.
- Whole-paper fit by default, plus zoom controls, edition navigation, keyboard navigation and browser Back/Forward support.

Only the original Friday movie has the newspaper section in this release. Halloween newspapers are not included or exposed; they are not being represented as finished. Existing movie information is unchanged.

UPLOAD ORDER — EXISTING GITHUB REPOSITORY

   Upload its assets folder at the repository root, keeping this exact structure:
   assets/newspapers/friday13/[six PNG files]

2. Unzip Comic-Vault-v10.57-App-Files.zip.
   Upload all .js and .css files from that ZIP to the repository root, where index.html already lives. Keep the filenames exactly as supplied.
   The older version numbers on unchanged companion files are intentional.

3. Upload index.html LAST, also at the repository root. This activates v10.57 only after the scripts, styles and six newspaper images are present.
   UPLOAD-INSTRUCTIONS.txt is for you; uploading it is optional.

4. Allow GitHub Pages to finish its normal deployment. Reload the site, using a hard refresh if needed, and confirm the visible version is v10.57.

You do not have to reconcile individual files from v10.55 or v10.56. The new index uses only the v10.57 newspaper JS/CSS. Old unused press files may remain; no deletion is required.

QUICK CHECK AFTER UPLOAD
- Open Friday the 13th (1980), then its detailed movie information.
- Choose Newspapers & Articles and unfold the archive.
- Open the newspaper. The entire page, including bottom ads, should fit without initial scrolling.
- Use the edition arrows/menu to visit all six papers.
- Tap a story or use Choose an article. The printed clipping enlarges; scroll/swipe to pan at larger zoom.
- Use Readable text for an exact transcript. Back to paper returns to the same edition.
- Escape returns from an article to its paper, then closes the newspaper. Browser Back/Forward preserves these layers.
